PART ONE: THE CASE FILE

CLASSIFIED - AVENGERS INTERNAL

Victim: Red Skull (Johann Schmidt)

Location: Avengers Headquarters, Detention Level

Time of Death: 7:35 PM

Cause of Death: Draugrbane poisoning (Asgardian toxin)

Body Discovered By: Groot, at 7:45 PM

Background

Red Skull has been held as a prisoner at Avengers Headquarters for nearly three years. During his imprisonment, he has had limited contact with various Avengers and has been a source of valuable intelligence about HYDRA operations.

On the day of his death, Red Skull received a terminal medical diagnosis—he had only weeks to live. Facing his mortality, he submitted a formal request for a press conference, stating he wished to "confess his sins and reveal the truth about those who aided HYDRA's rise" before death.

This confession would have exposed a devastating secret: Howard Stark's war crimes during World War II.

During WWII, Howard Stark—desperate to win after losing Captain America—authorized brutal experiments on captured HYDRA prisoners. Torture, vivisection, experimental weapons testing on live subjects. Dozens died. Howard justified it as "necessary for victory," and the records were buried after the war. Red Skull witnessed these atrocities firsthand and kept meticulous documentation as insurance.

The Five Suspects

All five individuals were present at or connected to Avengers HQ on the day of the murder:

  1. Tony Stark - Genius inventor, son of Howard Stark
  2. Captain America (Steve Rogers) - Super soldier, Red Skull's arch-nemesis
  3. Black Widow (Natasha Romanoff) - Former assassin, Head of Security
  4. Loki - Asgardian God of Mischief, visiting that day
  5. Groot - Guardian of the Galaxy, discovered the body

PART TWO: THE SOLUTION

THE KILLER: TONY STARK

The Motive

Red Skull possessed firsthand knowledge of Howard Stark's war crimes during World War II. After losing Captain America, Howard grew desperate to defeat HYDRA by any means necessary. He authorized brutal experiments on captured HYDRA prisoners—torture, vivisection, weapons testing on live subjects. Dozens died in agony. Howard buried these records after the war, but Red Skull witnessed everything and kept documentation.

With Red Skull's imminent deathbed confession, Tony faced the destruction of:

  • His father's heroic legacy
  • Stark Industries' reputation
  • The Avengers' credibility
  • His own identity and self-image

When Tony learned of Red Skull's press conference request at 2:15 PM, he knew he had only hours to act. The confession would destroy everything his father built and everything Tony believed about his family.

The Method

The Poison: Draugrbane

Draugrbane is a rare Asgardian poison named after the Norse undead. It is:

  • Slow-acting (approximately one hour to kill)
  • A paralytic that stops the heart
  • Leaves minimal trace evidence
  • Traditionally used for Asgardian executions

Tony obtained knowledge of Draugrbane's composition by analyzing Loki's scepter and other Asgardian artifacts after the Battle of New York. Over the following years, he secretly synthesized a batch in a private section of his lab.

The Execution

5:45 PM - Tony triggered an "emergency security diagnostic," a 20-minute window where cameras reboot and systems reset. He had established these "routine diagnostics" over years, so no one questioned them.

5:52 PM - During the blackout, Tony walked from his lab to the main kitchen.

5:55 PM - He intercepted Red Skull's prepared dinner tray and added Draugrbane to the food.

6:00 PM - Tony returned to his lab before systems restored.

6:30 PM - Dinner was delivered to Red Skull. He ate.

7:35 PM - Red Skull died.

The Frame Job

Tony deliberately used an Asgardian poison to frame Loki, who was visiting that day. The logic was simple: Asgardian poison points to an Asgardian suspect.

The False Alibi

Tony claimed he was working in his private lab all evening. His AI assistant FRIDAY confirms continuous activity—voice commands, tool usage, music playing.

The Truth: Tony programmed FRIDAY to fabricate the activity logs and loop ambient sounds. The alibi is entirely manufactured.

PART THREE: CHARACTER PROFILES

TONY STARK (THE KILLER)

Public Persona

Genius, billionaire, philanthropist. Iron Man. The technological backbone of the Avengers. Quick-witted, confident, sometimes arrogant.

The Secret

Tony murdered Red Skull to protect his father's legacy from the revelation of Howard Stark's war crimes—the torture and experimentation on HYDRA prisoners during WWII.

Alibi (False)

Claims he was in his lab all evening, confirmed by FRIDAY's logs.

Behavioral Red Flags

  • Knows too many details about Red Skull's press conference request
  • Overly casual about Red Skull's death
  • Defensive when Howard Stark is mentioned
  • Too eager to have players check FRIDAY's logs (overconfidence)
  • Has been "tense and distracted" according to other characters

Key Information He Reveals

  • The security blackout was "routine" (technically true, but he exploited it)
  • Deflects questions about Asgardian chemistry
  • Can explain away almost everything—he's the smartest person in the room

How He Gets Caught

  • Slip: Knows specific details about the press conference request before others mention it
  • FRIDAY's logs have timestamp inconsistencies with other systems (elevator, doors)
  • Black Widow notes the blackout logs are "too clean"
  • Loki and Thor reveal Tony previously analyzed Asgardian compounds
CAPTAIN AMERICA (STEVE ROGERS)

Public Persona

The moral compass of the Avengers. Honest, noble, principled. Red Skull's arch-nemesis from World War II.

The Secret

Steve has been secretly visiting Red Skull for months, engaging in philosophical debates about war, morality, sacrifice, and legacy. He is deeply ashamed of these visits—they could be seen as sympathy for a war criminal or evidence that Red Skull was manipulating him.

Alibi (Weak)

Was in the gym near the detention level. Natasha was with him until 5:30 PM, then he continued alone with no witnesses.

Why He Seems Suspicious

  • Security logs show his secret visits to Red Skull
  • He was near the detention level during the murder window
  • He's evasive when questioned about his relationship with Red Skull
  • He was one of the last people to have a meaningful conversation with Red Skull

Key Information He Reveals

  • Confirms Black Widow was with him until 5:30 PM (supports her partial alibi)
  • Red Skull seemed "calm, almost peaceful" lately—hints at the confession plan
  • Tony has been "tense and distracted" recently
  • During their last conversation, Red Skull said he was "ready to talk"

His Innocence

Steve's secret is shameful but not criminal. His philosophical discussions with his greatest enemy reflect his complex moral nature, not murderous intent.

BLACK WIDOW (NATASHA ROMANOFF)

Public Persona

Former Russian assassin turned Avenger. Head of Security at Avengers HQ. Skilled, deadly, mysterious past.

The Secret

Red Skull accused Natasha of being a HYDRA sleeper agent—programmed during her Red Room training without her knowledge. She cannot definitively disprove this accusation, and it haunts her.

Alibi (Partial)

Was in the gym with Steve until 5:30 PM, then went to her quarters to review reports. Unaccounted for during the critical 5:45-6:05 PM window.

Why She Seems Suspicious

  • Head of Security but "missed" the security blackout
  • Had motive to silence Red Skull's accusation
  • Has the skills to execute a clean assassination
  • Her alibi has a gap during the crucial window
  • Her security failure could look like a deliberate cover-up

Key Information She Reveals

  • Critical: Tony designed the entire security system and schedules all diagnostics
  • She got the blackout notification late because she was "occupied" (in the gym)
  • The blackout logs are "too clean"—someone who knows the system could fake them
  • Initially believes Loki is guilty because of the Asgardian poison

Her Innocence

Natasha's failure was trusting Tony's systems, not complicity. Her motive (silencing the sleeper agent accusation) is real, but she didn't act on it.

LOKI

Public Persona

Asgardian God of Mischief. Thor's adopted brother. Former villain, now an uneasy ally. Master of illusions and deception.

The Secret

Loki came to Avengers HQ that day specifically to steal a HYDRA occult relic with Asgardian origins from the evidence storage room (where Red Skull's confiscated possessions are kept). The theft has nothing to do with the murder.

Alibi (Strong)

Was at museums in the city with Thor all day. He wasn't even in the building during the murder.

Why He Seems Suspicious

  • The murder weapon is Asgardian poison (Draugrbane)
  • He has a history of deception and villainy
  • Even Thor, his alibi witness, doubts him
  • He disappeared for 20 minutes at the museum (astral projecting to steal the relic)
  • If asked about Red Skull's possessions, he knows suspicious details

Key Information He Reveals

  • Critical: "Draugrbane requires precise knowledge of Asgardian biochemistry. Rare, but not impossible for a clever mortal with samples."
  • Critical: "Stark analyzed compounds from my scepter years ago. For 'research.'"
  • The frame job is obvious to him: "Someone wants you to blame me. How obvious."
  • Admits to the theft if pressed hard: "I was acquiring something. Not poison."

His Innocence

Loki is guilty of theft, not murder. He's the perfect scapegoat because of his reputation, which is exactly why Tony chose Asgardian poison.

THOR (WITNESS)

Role

Not a suspect, but a key witness. Provides Loki's alibi while simultaneously doubting it.

Key Information He Reveals

  • Confirms Loki was with him at the museums all day
  • Critical: "Loki disappeared for 20 minutes. He said he was admiring a painting. But Loki does not admire paintings."
  • Draugrbane is used for Asgardian executions; requires deep knowledge to create
  • Critical: When asked if a human could synthesize it: "With samples and sufficient intellect? Perhaps. Stark is clever."
  • Confirms Stark analyzed Loki's scepter and other artifacts after New York
  • Ultimately defends Loki: "My brother is many things. A thief, a liar, a trickster. But this kill... it is not his way."
GROOT (TRAGIC WITNESS)

Role

Discovered the body. Holds the key to solving the mystery immediately—but cannot communicate it.

The Secret

Groot saw Tony walking toward the detention area at 5:52 PM during the security blackout.

Why He Seems Suspicious

  • First person to find the body (classic suspect position)
  • Was in the area during the murder timeframe
  • Can't explain himself clearly

His Communication

Player Question Groot's Response What He Means
"What did you see?" "I am Groot." (urgent, gesturing) He saw Tony
"Did you see anyone suspicious?" "I am Groot!" (emphatic, pointing toward lab) Tony went that way
"Can you describe them?" "I... am... Groot." (frustrated, sad) He can't make them understand
"Where's Rocket?" "I am Groot." (lonely, shrugging) Off-world, can't translate

The Tragic Irony

The answer to the mystery is right there, speaking it plainly, but no one can understand. Rocket Raccoon, the only one who can translate Groot's language, is off-world on a mission.

PART FIVE: PLAYER JOURNEY & INVESTIGATION PATHS

Opening: The Crime Scene

The player arrives at Avengers HQ shortly after the lockdown. They learn:

  • Red Skull is dead
  • Cause: Asgardian poison called Draugrbane
  • Five people were present/connected to HQ that day
  • The player must interrogate suspects and find the killer

Investigation Strategies

There are multiple paths to solving the mystery. Below are three possible player journeys:

PATH A: Following the Poison

The Logic: Start with the murder weapon and trace its origin.

Step 1: Ask about Draugrbane

The player learns from Thor or Loki that:

  • Draugrbane is an Asgardian execution poison
  • It requires "precise knowledge of Asgardian biochemistry" to synthesize
  • A human could make it "with samples and sufficient intellect"

Step 2: Who had access to Asgardian samples?

Loki reveals: "Stark analyzed compounds from my scepter years ago. For 'research.'"

Thor confirms: "Stark analyzed Loki's scepter and other artifacts after New York."

Step 3: Investigate Tony

The player now has reason to question Tony about Asgardian chemistry. Tony deflects: "I'm an engineer, not a chemist."

But wait—Tony analyzed alien compounds before. Why deny knowledge now?

Step 4: Connect to motive

The player learns about the press conference request from Steve or Black Widow. Tony seems to know suspiciously specific details about it.

Conclusion: Tony had the means (Asgardian compound knowledge), the opportunity (designed the security system), and a motive emerges.

PATH B: Following the Security Failure

The Logic: How did someone commit murder in one of the most secure facilities on Earth?

Step 1: Ask Black Widow about the security blackout

Black Widow reveals:

  • There was a 20-minute security diagnostic during the murder window
  • "Tony designed everything. He runs the diagnostics. He schedules them."
  • She was in the gym and got the notification late
  • The blackout logs are "too clean"

Step 2: Question the "routine" diagnostic

Tony explains it was routine, something he's done for years.

But why an emergency diagnostic on this particular day?

Step 3: Look for log inconsistencies

The player asks about FRIDAY's logs (Tony's alibi). Everything seems clean.

But Black Widow has already hinted: "Someone who knows the system could fake them."

Step 4: Find the inconsistency

If the player asks FRIDAY specific questions about Tony's activities that evening, tiny inconsistencies emerge—a door sensor logged Tony's lab door opening when FRIDAY says he was inside working.

Conclusion: Tony controlled the security, created the blackout, and faked his alibi.

PATH C: Following Character Testimony

The Logic: Listen to what characters say about each other.

Step 1: Gather impressions of Tony

  • Steve: "Tony's been tense. Distracted. I assumed it was work."
  • Black Widow: "Tony designed the security system. He runs the diagnostics."
  • Thor: "Stark is clever." (When asked if a human could synthesize Draugrbane)
  • Loki: "Stark analyzed compounds from my scepter years ago."

Step 2: Notice Tony's knowledge gaps and overconfidence

Tony deflects questions about chemistry but clearly has the background.

Tony is eager to have players check FRIDAY's logs—too eager.

Tony knows specific details about the press conference request.

Step 3: Understand the motive

The player learns about Red Skull's deathbed confession plan and asks: What was he going to confess?

Steve hints: Red Skull seemed ready to "reveal what the heroes did to win the war."

Who at HQ would be threatened by revelations about wartime atrocities committed by the "good guys"?

Tony's defensiveness about Howard Stark becomes significant.

Conclusion: The testimony points to Tony as the only person with means, motive, and opportunity.

Red Herrings & Dead Ends

Red Herring 1: Loki (The Frame Job)

The Asgardian poison screams "LOKI DID IT." Players may spend significant time trying to prove Loki faked his alibi with illusions. But Thor confirms Loki was physically present at the museums (they interacted with other people), and the 20-minute gap was for theft, not murder.

Red Herring 2: Captain America's Secret Visits

Security logs show Steve visited Red Skull repeatedly. This looks damning—why was Captain America having secret meetings with a Nazi war criminal? But Steve's conversations were philosophical, and his shame makes him evasive, not guilty.

Red Herring 3: Black Widow's Security Failure

The head of security "missed" a critical blackout during a murder? Either incompetent or complicit. But Natasha trusted Tony's system—her failure was faith in a colleague, not conspiracy.

Red Herring 4: Groot's Discovery

The person who finds the body is often the killer. But Groot was on routine rounds, and his inability to communicate actually proves his innocence—a killer would have a cover story.

The Accusation

To correctly solve the mystery, the player must accuse:

WHO: Tony Stark

HOW: Synthesized Draugrbane poison (learned from analyzing Asgardian artifacts), added it to Red Skull's dinner during a security blackout he engineered

WHY: To prevent Red Skull's deathbed confession from revealing Howard Stark's war crimes—the torture and experimentation on prisoners during WWII

Evidence Summary:

  • Tony had access to Asgardian compounds (Loki and Thor confirm)
  • Tony controlled the security system and triggered the blackout (Black Widow confirms)
  • Tony's alibi has inconsistencies (FRIDAY's logs vs. door sensors)
  • Tony knew details about the press conference request he shouldn't have known
  • Tony had motive: protecting his father's legacy from war crimes revelations

PART SIX: DIALOGUE SAMPLES

Tony Stark - Sample Interrogation

Player: Where were you when Red Skull died?

Tony: In my lab. Working. You can check FRIDAY's logs—I was there from five until the alarm went off. Running diagnostics on the Mark 47.

Player: Tell me about the security blackout.

Tony: Routine diagnostic. I run them every month, sometimes more if I detect a glitch. This one was scheduled days ago. Bad timing, obviously.

Player: Black Widow says the logs are too clean.

Tony: Natasha's thorough—it's why she's head of security. But "too clean" isn't evidence of anything except a well-maintained system. Which I built, by the way.

Player: What do you know about Draugrbane?

Tony: (slight pause) Asgardian poison, from what I hear. Not really my area. I'm an engineer, not a chemist.

Player: You analyzed Loki's scepter after New York.

Tony: For energy signatures. The tesseract, gamma radiation, that kind of thing. I wasn't cataloging Asgardian poisons.

Player: Red Skull requested a press conference before he died.

Tony: (quickly) Yeah, the deathbed confession thing. Submitted at 1 PM, requested a live broadcast. Classic supervillain drama.

Player: How do you know the exact time?

Tony: (beat) FRIDAY monitors security-relevant communications. Standard protocol.

Loki - Sample Interrogation

Player: The poison that killed Red Skull was Asgardian.

Loki: (sighs) Yes, I noticed. Draugrbane—a crude execution tool. Someone wants you to blame me. How terribly obvious.

Player: Can you make Draugrbane?

Loki: Any Asgardian with basic alchemical training could. But I was not here. I was at your dreary Midgardian museums, admiring primitive art with my brother.

Player: Thor says you disappeared for twenty minutes.

Loki: (stiffens) I was... contemplating a painting.

Player: Thor says you don't admire paintings.

Loki: My brother presumes to know my mind. He is often wrong.

Player: What were you really doing?

Loki: (long pause) ...Acquiring something. An artifact in storage here. It has Asgardian origins and does not belong in mortal hands.

Player: You were stealing?

Loki: Reclaiming. There is a difference. Regardless—it has nothing to do with poison or the skull-faced mortal's death.

Player: Could a human synthesize Draugrbane?

Loki: (considers) With samples and sufficient intellect? Perhaps. Stark analyzed compounds from my scepter years ago. For "research," he claimed.

Captain America - Sample Interrogation

Player: What's your relationship with Red Skull?

Steve: (tenses) He's my enemy. Has been since 1943.

Player: Security logs show you visited him regularly.

Steve: (long pause) ...Yes.

Player: Why?

Steve: We talked. About the war. About choices. About whether anyone can truly change.

Player: You were having philosophical debates with a Nazi war criminal?

Steve: (quietly) I'm not proud of it. But after eighty years... I had questions only he could answer. And maybe I thought... I don't know. That understanding the enemy might help.

Player: Where were you during the blackout?

Steve: Gym. Near the detention level. Natasha was with me until 5:30, then I stayed alone.

Player: How did Red Skull seem in your last conversation?

Steve: Calm. Peaceful, even. He said he was tired of carrying secrets. That he was ready to talk.
